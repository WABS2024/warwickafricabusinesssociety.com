Our Mission

To empower ambitious minds with the tools, network and practical insights to navigate the potential of Africa’s future business landscape

What is the Warwick African Business Society about?

The Warwick African Business Society (WABS) is the #1 fastest-growing business society on campus, dedicated to bridging the gap between students and the vast potential of the African business landscape.

We are a community of ambitious minds passionate about the continent's economic future, creating a space where cultural heritage meets corporate excellence. Whether you are African, of African descent, or simply fascinated by the continent's booming markets, WABS provides the platform to explore, connect, and lead.

What to expect from the Warwick African Business Society?

Membership with WABS is designed to accelerate your professional and personal growth through a dynamic, three-pillar experience.

Through our tailored events, reports, and community, you will gain the tools and connections necessary to navigate and thrive in a global business environment.

We provide Exclusive Insights into emerging African markets, host Unparalleled Networking events with industry leaders, and deliver Practical Knowledge that bridges the gap between university theory and real-world business success.
